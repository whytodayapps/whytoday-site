Pulse Support

Pulse is a decibel meter that keeps a record: it measures noise in dB(A), logs each session second by second and exports a PDF and CSV noise report. Questions, problems or ideas? Email whytodaysupport@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, its iOS or Android version, the Pulse version (Settings) and what happened. We usually reply within a few business days.

Readings

How accurate is Pulse?

Phone microphones vary. Typical accuracy is ±2 dB after calibration, from 30 to 120 dB. Pulse is for information only. It is not a certified sound level meter and not for legal, medical or occupational-compliance purposes.

Calibrate against a reference meter

Place your phone next to a sound level meter you trust, play a steady sound, and set Calibration Offset in Settings until both read the same. The offset is printed on every report.

The meter shows a dash

The meter is halted, for example while another app uses the microphone or during a phone call. Readings come back on their own when the microphone is free again. If they don't, check the microphone permission: Settings → Pulse → Microphone on iPhone, or Settings → Apps → Pulse → Permissions on Android.

Recording and reports

Tap record to start a session; tap Mark to flag a moment and add a note to it later in History. On Android a session keeps recording with the screen locked and shows a notification. On iPhone, keep Pulse open: a locked screen or a phone call shows as a gap in the log, and recording carries on when you come back. Open a session in History to export its PDF and CSV report. The SHA-256 value on the PDF can be recomputed from the CSV, so a reader can see the numbers were not retyped.

Pro (Android)

On Android, Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: no ads, unlimited sessions and reports, saved audio, compare 3 sessions and NIOSH / OSHA noise dose estimates. On iPhone all of this is included.

Restore a purchase

On a new phone or after reinstalling, open Settings and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Google account you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Google Play, and refunds are requested through your Google Play order history.

Privacy

Sound is analysed on your phone; audio and sound levels never leave it. Audio is saved only if you turn on Save audio with sessions. Read the full privacy policy, or email us about a privacy or data-deletion request.
