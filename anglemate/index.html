Measurements

The level does not read zero on a flat surface

Put the phone on the surface, wait a moment for it to settle, then tap Reset to use that position as zero. The reset lasts until you close the app. Phone cases and camera bumps can tilt the phone, so measure on a flat side without the case when accuracy matters.

How accurate is AngleMate?

Readings come from your phone's motion sensor and depend on the device and how it rests on the surface. AngleMate is a handy everyday tool, not a certified precision instrument; for critical work, check against a calibrated level.

The ruler is the wrong size

Open Ruler Measure, hold a real ruler against the screen and pinch to calibrate until the marks match. AngleMate remembers the calibration.

Camera (Laser Level)

Laser Level shows the rear camera with an alignment guide. It does not record photos or video and does not project a laser. If you declined camera access, allow it in your phone's settings: Settings → AngleMate → Camera on iPhone, or Settings → Apps → AngleMate → Permissions on Android.

Premium

What is Premium?

A one-time purchase that removes the ad banner from every tool once ads are introduced. The current version shows no ads. It is not a subscription.

Restore a purchase

On a new phone or after reinstalling, open the Premium tab and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play, and refunds are requested from them: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android through your Google Play order history. After a refund, Premium is removed the next time the app checks with the store.

Ads and privacy

The current version of AngleMate shows no ads. Before ads are introduced, we will update this page and the privacy policy.
